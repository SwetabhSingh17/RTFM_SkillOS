Slide 2: The Problem - Challenges in Capacity Building
- Rapid tech advancement (AI, Big Data, GIS) requires continuous upskilling.
- iGOT Karmayogi has vast resources, but officials struggle to find relevant courses.
- No intelligent mechanism for comprehensive skill-gap assessment based on specific job roles.
- Traditional training lacks personalization and continuous, real-time evaluation.

Slide 3: Our Solution - SkillOS (AI Learning Platform)
- An AI-powered Learning Management System (LMS) tailored for Official Statistics.
- Solves the "Cold Start" problem during onboarding by mapping 4 Metadata Vectors (Education, Role, Dept, Experience) to an Estimated Baseline Competency.
- Conducts automated skill-gap calculation (Target Role Profile minus Estimated Baseline) to generate immediate, targeted initial recommendations.
- Delivers highly personalized, adaptive learning pathways across 4 domains (Statistical, Technical, Digital Governance, Behavioural).

Slide 4: Key Innovation - Real-Time Orchestration System (RTOS)
- The "Central Nervous System" of our platform.
- Event-Driven Backbone: Captures course completions and quiz results instantly.
- Live Competency Sync: Recalculates skill scores and updates gaps in real-time.
- Dynamic Dashboards: Pushes updated recommendations directly to the Learner Dashboard via WebSockets without page reloads.

Slide 5: Data & Process Workflow
- Step 1 (Ingestion): User metadata (role, dept) and learning materials (PDFs, Videos) are ingested.
- Step 2 (Processing): The AI Engine maps profiles to baselines, while background workers parse documents to generate MCQs.
- Step 3 (Analysis): The Gap Calculation Engine compares actual scores against Target Job Role requirements.
- Step 4 (Action): The Semantic Recommender matches these specific gaps against the iGOT/NSSTA course index.
- Step 5 (Feedback Loop): Once a user completes a task, the RTOS instantly recalculates their score and restarts the cycle.

Slide 6: Seamless Ecosystem Integration
- Deep integration with iGOT Karmayogi APIs and NSSTA's TPAC.
- Intelligent Course Matching: Uses semantic search to map identified skill gaps to the exact courses needed.
- Progress Tracking: Automatically monitors enrollments, learning hours, and updates competency scores upon course completion.
- Secure & Interoperable: Built with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Single Sign-On (SSO) aligned with Government guidelines.

Slide 7: AI-Powered Assessment Engine
- Automated Quiz Generation: Trainers upload PDFs, PPTXs, or Video transcripts.
- AI (LLMs/NLP) instantly parses the content, chunks it, and generates objective MCQs.
- Includes plausible distractors and detailed explanations for self-evaluation.
- Processed in the background via asynchronous task queues (Redis) so the UI remains fluid.

Slide 8: Impact & Future Readiness
- For Learners: A clear, customized roadmap to mastering modern statistical and digital skills.
- For Administrators: Interactive heatmaps, training effectiveness metrics, and predictive analytics for workforce planning.
- Ultimate Goal: Building a data-driven, future-ready statistical workforce equipped for the evolving needs of India.
